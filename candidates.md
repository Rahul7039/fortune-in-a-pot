# Fortune in a Pot — candidate scoring

Judge weighs, in order: (1) fortune for a houseplant, (2) funny in a plant-lover way
(small joke that lands, not a groaned pun), (3) kind/warm, no doom/withering/mockery,
(4) sounds like a fortune (prediction cadence, not slogan/caption/care instruction),
(5) one line ≤120 chars, reads cleanly aloud, no padding. Specific to THIS plant in
THIS window beats generic.

|---|-------|-----------|---------|
| 1 | 105 | The cat will sniff you, decide you are not food, and by Friday you will grow a new leaf just to show off. | **CHOSEN.** Prediction shape (will… will…), windowsill-specific (cat, new leaf), real joke that lands, kind, punch ("just to show off") at the end. |

"The cat will sniff you, decide you are not food, and by Friday you will grow a new leaf just to show off."
